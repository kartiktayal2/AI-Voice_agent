# Sample Bank — Personal Loan Knowledge Base

## 1. Personal Loan Overview

A personal loan is a loan that an eligible applicant can apply for, subject to the bank's eligibility verification and approval process.

The sample bank provides personal loans within the following limits:

- Minimum loan amount: ₹50,000.
- Maximum loan amount: ₹10,00,000.
- Starting interest rate: 10.5% per annum.
- Maximum repayment tenure: 7 years.
- Processing fee: 1% of the approved loan amount.

The actual loan amount, applicable interest rate, repayment tenure, and other terms depend on the approved loan and its applicable terms.

## 2. Personal Loan Amount

The personal loan amount ranges from ₹50,000 to ₹10,00,000.

The minimum loan amount is ₹50,000.

The maximum loan amount is ₹10,00,000.

An applicant should not assume that the maximum amount will automatically be approved. Loan approval depends on eligibility verification and the bank's approval process.

An applicant must meet the following stated eligibility requirements:

1. The applicant must be at least 21 years old.
2. The applicant must have a regular source of income.
3. The applicant must have a valid PAN card.
4. The applicant must provide valid identity proof.
5. The applicant must provide valid address proof.

Meeting these stated requirements does not guarantee loan approval. The application remains subject to eligibility verification and approval.

The maximum eligible age, minimum income, employment duration, credit score requirements, and other detailed eligibility criteria are not specified in the available policy information.
