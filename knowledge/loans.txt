Personal Loan

The personal loan amount ranges from ₹50,000 to ₹10,00,000.

The interest rate for a personal loan starts from 10.5% per annum.

The maximum repayment tenure for a personal loan is 7 years.

The processing fee is 1% of the approved loan amount.


Personal Loan Eligibility

The applicant must be at least 21 years old.

The applicant must have a regular source of income.

The applicant must have a valid PAN card.

The applicant must provide valid identity and address proof.


Loan Application

Customers can apply for a personal loan by submitting the required application and documents.

The application is subject to eligibility verification and approval.

Loan approval is not guaranteed and depends on the applicant's eligibility and verification.


Loan Repayment

Customers must repay the approved loan according to the agreed repayment schedule.

The exact monthly installment depends on the loan amount, interest rate, and repayment tenure.
